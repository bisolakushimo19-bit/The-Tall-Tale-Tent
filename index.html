THE BARKER EXPLAINS

How to play

I can juggleI met a mayorI've been to 31 countries

1 Write two truths and one lie

You get 90 seconds to write three short tales about yourself. Mark the one that's a lie. Stuck? Tap a prompt chip for a starter.

AB ✓C

2 Spot the whopper

Each player steps into the spotlight. Their tales appear as tickets A, B and C in a shuffled order. Tap the one you think is the lie and lock it in. Right: +100. Fool people with your own tales: +50 for each one.

☼ Work the spotlight. Once per game, on your own turn, swing the spotlight onto one ticket. It might be a hint, or a bluff.

×2

3 Bet a Go Double chip when you're sure

You get one brass Go Double chip per game (two in games of 10 or more). Right with a chip: +200. Wrong with a chip: −50.

🎯 Last Call. Before the reveal, the tote board shows how the crowd bet, and you can switch. If you were already on the whopper while it held under half the bets, you score a long-shot bonus of +50.

Tales live only in the tent's memory and are deleted when the tent closes. No accounts, nothing stored.
